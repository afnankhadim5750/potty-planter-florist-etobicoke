Our story

A family flower shop, rooted in Etobicoke since 1971.

The Potty Planter Florist, also known as The Potty Planter & Friends, has served Etobicoke for over 40 years. It is a family owned and operated shop, and that shows in the way orders are put together: carefully, personally, and with an eye for presentation.

Customers come back again and again, for birthdays and anniversaries, for weddings, and for the harder days too. Whether it is a dozen roses or a dish garden for a neighbour, every arrangement leaves the shop ready to be given.

Questions

Before you order

Do you deliver in Etobicoke?

Yes, delivery is available in the local area, and orders can also be picked up from the shop at 1500 Royal York Road. Delivery charges are not included in the listed prices, so ask when you order.

Can I order wedding flowers?

Yes, wedding flowers are one of the shop's services. Call (416) 245-7300 to talk through your date, colours and plans.

Do you carry gifts as well as flowers?

Yes. The shop carries baby gifts, stuffed animals, candy and chocolate, greeting cards, and silk and artificial flowers, alongside fresh arrangements.

What are your hours?

The public listing shows the shop open 10am to 6pm on the day checked. Please call (416) 245-7300 to confirm today's hours before visiting.

How do I place an order?

Call (416) 245-7300, email thepottyplanter@rogers.com, or use the order form below. Tell the shop the occasion, the recipient and the delivery or pickup details.
